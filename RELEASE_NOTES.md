Quick notes, readable Markdown, and small to-dos in your Mac menu bar.

- Keep notes on two Macs with an optional shared iCloud Drive folder. Work offline and retain conflicting edits as separate notes.
- Import Markdown, export all active notes, and create or restore complete Slip backups. Local snapshots protect previously saved notes.
- Keep Slip ready at login, check for published updates, and download a new version when you choose.
- Return to the same panel size and writing position. Jump to matching search text and undo a note deletion.
- Complete tasks with Command-Return, indent list items with Tab, and paste a URL over selected text to create a link.

The folder sync implementation is covered by local replica simulations; transfer between two physical Macs and login-after-reboot have not yet been verified.

Download the DMG, open it, and drag **Slip** to **Applications** in the window that appears. Open Slip from Applications, then eject the disk image. See the [README](https://github.com/berkinduz/slip-menu-note-app#install) for installation and the [user guide](https://github.com/berkinduz/slip-menu-note-app/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md) for usage and shortcuts.

**Compatibility:** macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon. This build is ad hoc signed and is not notarized; macOS may show a first-launch warning. Apple's [opening-app guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) explains the available options.

Your existing notes, folders, tags, and settings are preserved when replacing the app.

Contributions, bug reports, and focused UI improvements are welcome.
