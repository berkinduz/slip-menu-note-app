Quick notes and small to-dos, one shortcut away.

- Open from the menu bar or with **Control–Option–N**. Start writing without a title.
- Read and edit Markdown in one place, with hidden formatting markers and colored highlight.
- Use clear, clickable task boxes and `/` commands while you work.
- Organize notes with manual tags, nested groups, optional monochrome icons, and a quiet tag footer.
- Choose your font and appearance. Kanagawa Dragon and SF Pro Text are the defaults.
- Keep notes locally, export Markdown, pin notes, and restore from Trash.

Download the ZIP, unzip it, and move `Slip.app` to Applications. See the [README](https://github.com/berkinduz/slip-menu-note-app#install) for usage and shortcuts.

**Compatibility:** macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon. This build is ad hoc signed and is not notarized; macOS may show a first-launch security warning. Apple's [opening-app guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) explains the available options.

Contributions, bug reports, and focused feature proposals are welcome.
